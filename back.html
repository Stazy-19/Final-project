Based on your hea;th and body goals mix match the workouts below and do them within the rep/set that apply to you. • Muscle Growth (Hypertrophy / "Toning"): 6 to 12 reps per set (often 8–12 as a sweet spot for women) • Weight: Moderate-heavy loads where you are near muscle failure by the final rep (leaving 1–2 reps in reserve). • Rest: 1 to 2 minutes between sets. • Focus: Creates mechanical tension and metabolic stress to shape and build lean muscle. • Muscular Endurance & Conditioning: 15 to 20+ reps per set • Weight: Lighter loads. • Rest: 30 to 60 seconds between sets. • Focus: Keeps the heart rate elevated and improves stamina. • Fat Loss: 10 to 15 reps per set (combined with high-density training) • Weight: Moderate resistance. • Rest: 30 seconds to 1 minute to maintain metabolic burn. • Focus: Preserves muscle mass while burning calories during a caloric deficit.

Here are a few excersises for Back • Latissimus Dorsi (Lats): Location: Large muscles on the sides and mid-back that create the classic V-shape or upper-body taper. Best Exercises: Lat pulldowns, pull-ups (or assisted pull-ups), and dumbbell pullovers. • Trapezius (Traps): Location: Spans the back of the neck, shoulders, and mid-back. Best Exercises: Face pulls, resistance band pull-aparts, and upright rows. • Rhomboids: Location: Located right between your shoulder blades. Best Exercises: Seated cable rows, single-arm dumbbell rows, and bent-over rows. • Erector Spinae (Lower Back): Location: Runs vertically down both sides of your spine. Best Exercises: Conventional deadlifts, Romanian deadlifts, bodyweight Supermans, and back extensions.
