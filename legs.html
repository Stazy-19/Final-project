different goals means different reps and sets. Based on your hea;th and body goals mix match the workouts below and do them within the rep/set that apply to you. • Muscle Growth (Hypertrophy / "Toning"): 6 to 12 reps per set (often 8–12 as a sweet spot for women) • Weight: Moderate-heavy loads where you are near muscle failure by the final rep (leaving 1–2 reps in reserve). • Rest: 1 to 2 minutes between sets. • Focus: Creates mechanical tension and metabolic stress to shape and build lean muscle. • Muscular Endurance & Conditioning: 15 to 20+ reps per set • Weight: Lighter loads. • Rest: 30 to 60 seconds between sets. • Focus: Keeps the heart rate elevated and improves stamina. • Fat Loss: 10 to 15 reps per set (combined with high-density training) • Weight: Moderate resistance. • Rest: 30 seconds to 1 minute to maintain metabolic burn. • Focus: Preserves muscle mass while burning calories during a caloric deficit.

here are some Exercises for legs and lowerbody: Quadriceps (Front of Thighs) • Anatomy: Four muscles on the front of the thigh that extend the knee. Women are often naturally quad-dominant, so form adjustments can help balance the load • Best Exercises: • Barbell or Goblet Squats • Walking or Reverse Lunges • Bulgarian Split Squats (closer foot stance) • Leg Extensions (isolation) 2. Hamstrings (Back of Thighs) • Anatomy: Three muscles running down the back of the thigh that bend the knee and extend the hip. Strengthening these helps protect the knees and create a balanced leg shape. • Best Exercises: • Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs) • Seated or Lying Leg Curls • Single-Leg Romanian Deadlifts 3. Glutes (Buttocks & Hips) • Anatomy: Comprises the gluteus maximus, medius, and minimus. They power hip extension, stabilization, and shaping. • Best Exercises: • Barbell Hip Thrusts • Sumo Squats • Curtsy Lunges • Glute Bridges 5. Adductors and Abductors (Inner & Outer Thighs) • Anatomy: Stabilizers that control side-to-side leg movement and hip alignment. • Best Exercises: • Lateral Lunges (Side Lunges) • Hip Adductor / Abductor Machines • Banded Lateral Monster Walks
