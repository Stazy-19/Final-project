core

muscle groups within core are front core, side core, back core, and the top and bottom stabilizers.

different goals means different reps and sets. Based on your hea;th and body goals mix match the workouts below and do them within the rep/set that apply to you. • Muscle Growth (Hypertrophy / "Toning"): 6 to 12 reps per set (often 8–12 as a sweet spot for women) • Weight: Moderate-heavy loads where you are near muscle failure by the final rep (leaving 1–2 reps in reserve). • Rest: 1 to 2 minutes between sets. • Focus: Creates mechanical tension and metabolic stress to shape and build lean muscle. • Muscular Endurance & Conditioning: 15 to 20+ reps per set • Weight: Lighter loads. • Rest: 30 to 60 seconds between sets. • Focus: Keeps the heart rate elevated and improves stamina. • Fat Loss: 10 to 15 reps per set (combined with high-density training) • Weight: Moderate resistance. • Rest: 30 seconds to 1 minute to maintain metabolic burn. • Focus: Preserves muscle mass while burning calories during a caloric deficit.

here are few excersises that touch on each group for arms: Biceps Brachii (Front Upper Arm): Bends the elbow and rotates the forearm. It has two heads: the short head (on the inner/front side, targeted with elbows in front of the body) and the long head (contributes to the peak, targeted with elbows at your sides or behind you). Brachialis (Deep Front Upper Arm): Sits underneath the biceps and assists with elbow flexion. Developing it pushes the biceps upward to add overall arm thickness. Triceps Brachii (Back Upper Arm): Makes up about two-thirds of your upper arm mass and straightens the elbow. It has three heads: the long head, lateral head, and medial head. Forearm Muscles (Lower Arm): Composed of flexors and extensors that manage wrist movement, finger control, and grip strength.

Here are a few excersises for Back • Latissimus Dorsi (Lats): Location: Large muscles on the sides and mid-back that create the classic V-shape or upper-body taper. Best Exercises: Lat pulldowns, pull-ups (or assisted pull-ups), and dumbbell pullovers. • Trapezius (Traps): Location: Spans the back of the neck, shoulders, and mid-back. Best Exercises: Face pulls, resistance band pull-aparts, and upright rows. • Rhomboids: Location: Located right between your shoulder blades. Best Exercises: Seated cable rows, single-arm dumbbell rows, and bent-over rows. • Erector Spinae (Lower Back): Location: Runs vertically down both sides of your spine. Best Exercises: Conventional deadlifts, Romanian deadlifts, bodyweight Supermans, and back extensions.

cardio

here are different cardio options depednig on your health goals: Fat Loss & Metabolism Boost • Best Practice: High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT) combined with strength training. • Why: Alternating short bursts of maximum effort (like sprint intervals or HIIT bikes) with recovery burns significant calories quickly and elevates your metabolic rate post-workout. • Heart Health & Stress Reduction (Cortisol Management) • Best Practice: Zone 2 steady-state cardio (brisk walking, light cycling, or swimming). • Why: Keeping your heart rate at 60–70% of its maximum allows you to hold a conversation while building your aerobic base, improving mitochondrial function, and keeping stress hormones like cortisol balanced. • Joint Protection & Longevity • Best Practice: Low-impact steady-state options like swimming, cycling, or incline walking. • Why: These deliver strong cardiovascular and post-menopausal heart benefits without placing heavy shock-loading stress on the ankles, knees, or hips. • Total-Body Toning & Muscle Preservation • Best Practice: Circuit training or full-body ergometers (rowing, stair climbing). • Why: Rowing and circuit routines engage both upper and lower muscle groups simultaneously, merging aerobic conditioning with muscular endurance.
