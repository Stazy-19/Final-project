Core

Muscle groups within core are front core, side core, back core, and the top and bottom stabilizers.

Different goals means different reps and sets. Based on your hea;th and body goals mix match the workouts below and do them within the rep/set that apply to you.

Muscle Growth (Hypertrophy / "Toning"): 6 to 12 reps per set (often 8–12 as a sweet spot for women) Weight: Moderate-heavy loads where you are near muscle failure by the final rep (leaving 1–2 reps in reserve). Rest: 1 to 2 minutes between sets. Focus: Creates mechanical tension and metabolic stress to shape and build lean muscle.

Muscular Endurance & Conditioning: 15 to 20+ reps per set Weight: Lighter loads. Rest: 30 to 60 seconds between sets. Focus: Keeps the heart rate elevated and improves stamina.

Fat Loss: 10 to 15 reps per set (combined with high-density training) Weight: Moderate resistance. Rest: 30 seconds to 1 minute to maintain metabolic burn. Focus: Preserves muscle mass while burning calories during a caloric deficit.
